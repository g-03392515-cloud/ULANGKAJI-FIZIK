SENARAI RUMUS FIZIK KSSM TINGKATAN 4

Bab 2: Daya dan Gerakan I & II

• $v = u + at$
• $s = \frac{1}{2}(u + v)t$
• $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
• $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$
• Momentum: $p = mv$
• Keabadian Momentum: $m_1 u_1 + m_2 u_2 = m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2$
• Daya Bersih: $F = ma$
• Daya Impuls: $F = \frac{mv - mu}{t}$

Bab 3: Kegravitian

• Hukum Newton: $F = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}$
• Pecutan Graviti: $g = \frac{GM}{r^2}$
• Daya Memusat: $F_c = \frac{mv^2}{r}$
• Laju Linear Satelit: $v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$
• Hukum Kepler III: $\frac{T_1^2}{T_2^2} = \frac{r_1^3}{r_2^3}$
• Halaju Lepas: $v = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}$

Bab 4: Haba

• Muatan Haba Tentu: $Q = mc\Delta\theta$
• Haba Pendam Tentu: $Q = ml$
• Kuasa Elektrik: $Pt = mc\Delta\theta = ml$
• Hukum Gas: $\frac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}$

Bab 5 & 6: Gelombang, Cahaya & Optik

• Laju Gelombang: $v = f\lambda$
• Interferens: $\lambda = \frac{ax}{D}$
• Indeks Biasan: $n = \frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{c}{v} = \frac{1}{\sin c}$
• Formula Kanta: $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{u} + \frac{1}{v}$
• Pembesaran Linear: $m = \frac{v}{u} = \frac{h_i}{h_o}$
